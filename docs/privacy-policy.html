Privacy Policy

Flip 7 Score Tracker · Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flip 7 Score Tracker ("the app") handles information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Information We Collect

Flip 7 Score Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the app — player names, scores, rounds, and your chosen settings and theme — is stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
